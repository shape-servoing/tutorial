Beyond planar shape servoing

The loop does not depend on the object being a planar rod. In three dimensions, tracked points contribute three feature coordinates, robot coordinates include spatial translations and rotations, and the deformation Jacobian still maps robot motion to feature motion:

\dot{\vs}=\vJ(\vq)\vu,\qquad \vu=\alpha\,\vJ^{+}\big(\vs^{*}-\vs\big).

Spatial rod. A centerline in \R^3 captures bending in two planes and a change of tip direction. A cubic centerline joins two short, rigid grasp regions, so the actuation has a direct end-effector interpretation.

Planar sheet. A triangular surface mesh represents a thin object. Its equilibrium minimizes an as-rigid-as-possible (ARAP) energy, which assigns a local rotation \mathbf R_i to every vertex:

E_{\mathrm{ARAP}}=\sum_{(i,j)\in\mathcal E}w_{ij}\norm{(\vx_i-\vx_j)-\mathbf R_i(\vx_i^0-\vx_j^0)}^2.

Each gripper constrains an anchor vertex and its graph neighbors within topological radius one. These compact, connected patches approximate finite-area contacts without unrealistically clamping an entire boundary. Three coordinates move and twist the actuated patch; local-global ARAP iterations propagate that motion while approximately preserving each triangle's local shape.

Volumetric solid. Surface triangles alone do not describe the interior of a solid. Here ARAP acts on a 3-D lattice, including its interior vertices, while the triangulated boundary is rendered as a translucent surface. Local patches on opposing sides support compression, translation, and twist.

Try this

  1. Drag the main canvas to orbit each object, then compare its front, top, and side projections. The deliberately pronounced target deformation, ground grid, and shadows make the spatial displacement easier to judge.
  2. Select Spatial rod and run: right-gripper lift, depth motion, and tip direction converge together.
  3. Select ARAP sheet, increase the target deformation, and run. The orange grasp patch reaches its target while the triangular mesh distributes the bend smoothly.
  4. Select Volumetric solid and switch on show volume lattice. The boundary and interior deform together rather than treating the visible surface as a hollow sheet.
  5. Use Step once to relate generalized actuation and command size to error reduction. Watch the Jacobian heat map, its singular values, rank, and condition number as the local linearization changes.

Changing object class changes the perception and deformation model, but not the shape-servoing architecture: measure a feature error, estimate or compute its Jacobian with respect to robot motion, and close the loop.

What this demo computes

The sheet has 35 vertices and 48 triangles. The refined solid uses a 150-vertex volumetric lattice and renders its 224 boundary triangles. Grasp patches are graph balls: all vertices reachable from an anchor in at most one mesh edge. Refinement therefore reduces the physical size of the solid's contact without shrinking the object. Both ARAP models alternate batched local rotation fitting with a constrained global position solve. The controller stacks selected vertex positions into \vs, evaluates \vJ=\partial\vs/\partial\vq by centered differences, and applies a bounded pseudoinverse step. Targets are generated by the same equilibrium model, so all three are reachable.
